# Ourea — competitive / adjacent landscape

## Core positioning

> **Medellín already has risk intelligence. OUREA turns it into adaptation investment intelligence.**

That sentence prevents the product from competing in an already mature local space.

## SIATA

Role:
- hydrometeorological monitoring;
- rainfall/weather data;
- early-warning infrastructure.

OUREA relationship:
**input/complement, not replacement**.

## Inform@Risk / Bello Oriente

Published Medellín work demonstrates participatory local landslide early warning, monitoring and institutional/community processes.

Source:
`https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/24/1843/`

OUREA distinction:
physical adaptation portfolio decisions rather than another sensor/alert system.

## Sapena et al. 2023

Published citywide Medellín workflow combines susceptibility/exposure with cost-effectiveness to prioritize **local landslide early-warning instrumentation**.

Source:
`https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/23/3913/2023/`

Closest conceptual adjacent work.

Their decision:
> where should monitoring/LEWS instrumentation be deployed under limited funds?

OUREA decision:
> which physical adaptation portfolio should be tested/funded under uncertain climate/effectiveness and explicit social/access priorities?

## DUCTExplorer — 2024 Innovate4Cities winner

Official Innovate4Cities materials emphasize the path from hackathon tool to actionable city pilot.

Source:
`https://www.innovate4cities.org/ai-x-city-climate-action/`

Lesson:
- actionable city decision output matters;
- pilotability matters;
- a strong geospatial interface is useful, but 3D alone is not the innovation.

## Polisense.AI / EcoGuardian.AI — 2025 winners

`https://www.innovate4cities.org/blog/winners-2025-ai-city-climate-action-hackathon-at-climate-week-nyc/`

## Ourea differentiation stack

1. **Decision target:** adaptation investment rather than detection/warning.
2. **City scale:** Medellín city shortlist + detailed proving ground.
3. **Physical action:** three intervention families.
4. **Uncertainty:** climate + intervention effects.
5. **Policy transparency:** four named objective lenses.
6. **Robustness:** P10/median/P90, budget frontier, stability.
7. **Multiobjective:** sampled equity/access trade-offs.
8. **Explainability:** project-level “Why here?”
9. **Evidence discipline:** official vs proxy vs prior.
10. **Auditability:** exportable decision package.
11. **Validation path:** SIATA historical hindcast.
12. **Formal cross-check:** MILP + nonlinear reevaluation.

## Claims to avoid

- “AI predicts landslides in real time.”
- “Medellín needs a warning system.”
- “We replace SIATA.”
- “We built a digital twin” as the headline.
- “This is the optimal public investment plan.”
- “The equity score measures poverty.”
- “Our P10 is a guaranteed real-world impact.”
- “This sampled plot is the exact Pareto frontier.”
