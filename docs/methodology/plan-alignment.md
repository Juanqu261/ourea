# Comuna 8 plan alignment

This layer traces **published** local planning and research to Ourea intervention families.

It is documentary alignment, not community endorsement, not a numeric local calibration, and not a substitute for co-design.

## Scope

- Geographic focus: Comuna 8 / Villa Hermosa, Medellín.
- Current proving ground remains Llanaditas / upper Comuna 8.
- Moravia is not a target of this registry.

## What each entry records

- related plan action or research line;
- intervention type, or cross-cutting if none;
- source URL;
- geographic scope;
- evidence status (`documentary` or `research`);
- prerequisites before treating the action as implementation-ready;
- maintenance responsibility, only when a source documents it;
- remaining evidence or validation gap.

## What it does not do

- invent community partners, consultations or acceptance scores;
- alter optimizer rankings;
- convert literature into local cost or effect parameters.

Canonical file: `frontend/public/data/plan_alignment.json`.
