# Community co-design protocol

This protocol is a pilot design, not a record of completed participation. Ourea has **no invented partners, organizations or participation results**.

Moravia’s lesson is that technically justified works can fail socially if attachment, livelihoods, trust and maintenance are ignored. The protocol keeps those issues visible without turning them into fake scores.

## Who may record, review and approve

To be agreed in Month 1 with the city counterpart and legitimate community representatives:
- who may enter participatory scenario input;
- who may review it;
- who may mark a consultation as `validated`;
- how a resident can contest a record;
- how data leave the session.

Until that agreement exists, session values remain labeled **participatory scenario input**.

## What Ourea must not do

- predict community acceptance;
- recommend resettlement;
- treat IMCV or stratum 1 as proof of consent;
- average incompatible positions into one “community weight”;
- silently drop opposed projects from a technically generated portfolio.

## From notes to future constraints

Only after a facilitated session may an **explicit, written** criterion become a transparent constraint, for example:
- “no intervention that requires displacement in this cell”;
- “preserve this access path used for care and work”.

Those constraints would be documented in the decision package. They are not encoded in the current optimizer.
