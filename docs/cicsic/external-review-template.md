# External review template (optional)

Intended for 3–5 independent experts or practitioners. **Do not** fill with fabricated responses.

## Context to provide reviewers

- Two cases: Medellín (deep local evidence) and Nanjing/Xianlin (portability).
- Shared decision engine; city-specific adapters/data/lenses.
- Outputs are **planning-proxy** portfolio scores under uncertainty — not hydraulic flood models.
- Municipal retrospective projects are **not** optimizer inputs.

## Questions

1. Is the decision problem (allocate limited planning credits across interventions under uncertainty) understandable from the materials alone?

2. Are the portfolio outputs (selected interventions, P10 / expected scores, assumptions) interpretable for a non-modeler?

3. Are key assumptions (costs in credits, effect ranges, missing equity data in Nanjing, climate product choice) sufficiently visible?

4. Would comparing a **robust** portfolio to a **deterministic/central** portfolio be useful in a real planning discussion? Why or why not?

5. What information is missing before you would consider this usable alongside professional planning tools?

6. Could this support — but not replace — professional judgment in adaptation screening? What boundaries would you set?

## Rules for facilitators

- Do not ask leading questions such as “Would you agree Ourea is superior to existing tools?”
- Do not coach reviewers toward confirming competition claims.
- Record disagreements and limitations verbatim.
- Separate Medellín validity questions from Nanjing portability questions.
