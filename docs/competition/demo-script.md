# Ourea — 2-minute demo

Public demo: https://ybedoyab.github.io/ourea/

Default path: **Guided decision**. Optional: **Load completed example** for a fixed-seed Review. **Explore freely** remains in the More menu.

## 0:00–0:20 — What this is

The first question is **Where should the city act?**

“Ourea is an evidence-backed decision tool that helps cities turn climate risk, budget constraints, equity and community safeguards into robust portfolios of urban resilience actions.”

## 0:20–0:40 — Where is the risk?

Switch lenses: **Exposure → Balanced → Equity**.

“Same official hazard, 2026 population and IMCV layers. Three planning weights — not three datasets. 271 spatial polygons, 249 official urban records, 248/249 population matches. Rankings start at #1, not unmatched sites.”

“Llanaditas is the detailed proving ground, not rank #1 under every lens.”

Click **Analyze Llanaditas**.

## 0:40–1:00 — What conditions and what matters?

**What conditions should we plan for?** Typical wet, High rainfall or Extreme observed, plus planning credits.

**What should the portfolio prioritize?** Balanced, Equity-first, Access-first or Low-regret.

## 1:00–1:20 — How is the portfolio built?

Click **Generate recommendation**.

“Ourea tests 125 intervention-location candidates across uncertainty and the selected policy priority. The same budget and seed, four public-policy weights.”

## 1:20–1:40 — Does this portfolio hold up?

Read P10 in plain language, then **Compare on map**: No action / Ourea.

Open **Advanced analysis** only if a judge wants benchmark, breakage, frontier or trade-offs. Optionally click **Generate decision review** — that explains the existing result; it does not approve construction.

## 1:40–2:00 — Is this decision ready to discuss?

Evidence, community review and local alignment sit behind short cards. Click **Download PDF** — a 6–8-page brief with a US$ envelope, cell links and an illustrative early-action mechanism animation, not a site-specific collapse forecast.

“Don’t just show me where climate risk is. Let me test what Medellín can do about it — and see which choices stay robust enough to take to residents.”

**Ourea.**
